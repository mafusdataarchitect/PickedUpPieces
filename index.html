About the Band

High Energy. Great Songs. Built for the Crowd.

Picked Up Pieces is a modern, high-energy cover band based in Sandpoint, Idaho, performing the best popular music from the past 30 years. Our sound blends 90s alternative, 2000s pop and indie, 2010s chart-toppers, and today's most influential artists.

We focus on recognizable, crowd-pleasing songs performed with tight rhythms, dynamic arrangements, and a polished stage presence. Whether it's a relaxed afternoon at a lakeside venue in Hope or a packed evening at a Sandpoint winery, we deliver a show that feels fresh, contemporary, and genuinely fun.

We're proud to be part of the North Idaho music scene — playing benefits, local events, and the venues that make this region special.

Live Performance

A Show Built Around the Moment

Picked Up Pieces typically performs three sets plus an encore — 2.5 to 3 hours of live music. We tailor every performance to the venue, reading the room and adjusting energy accordingly.

  • Clean, coordinated song intros and transitions
  • Dynamic control — from brushed ballads to full-band anthems
  • Electronic textures and percussion variety
  • Smooth energy arcs — mellow, groove-driven, and high-impact
  • Polished stage presence and crowd engagement
